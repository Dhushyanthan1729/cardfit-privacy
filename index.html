CardFit Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 September 2026

CardFit reads the transaction messages your bank sends you, works out where your money goes, and recommends credit cards that reward that spending. This policy explains exactly what stays on your phone, what leaves it, and why.

The short version. Your SMS messages are read and analysed entirely on your device and are never uploaded. What does leave your phone is your Google account email, a small set of product usage events, and anonymised category spending totals (for example "Food: ₹68,000 over 12 months") used to generate card recommendations. Individual transactions, merchant names and message contents never leave your device.

Who we are

What stays on your device

The following is processed locally and never transmitted anywhere:

  • The content of your SMS messages
  • Individual transactions — amounts, dates, merchant or payee names
  • Your account and card numbers, balances and available credit limits
  • Category assignments you make manually

This data is stored in an app-private database on your phone. Uninstalling CardFit deletes it permanently.

Third parties

Google Firebase handles authentication and stores usage events. Governed by the Firebase privacy documentation.

Anthropic processes anonymised category totals to generate recommendations. See the Anthropic privacy policy.

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers.

Affiliate links

When you tap "Apply" on a recommended card, you may be sent to a partner link that earns CardFit a commission if your application is approved. This costs you nothing extra and never influences which cards we show you — recommendations are generated from your spending, and the app labels partner links where they appear.

Accuracy of recommendations

Reward rates, caps and fees are estimates generated by an AI model and change often. They are a starting point for your own research, not a quote. Always confirm current terms on the card issuer's own website before applying. Applying for a credit card creates a hard enquiry on your credit report, and several applications in quick succession can lower your credit score.

Retention and deletion

On-device data lives until you uninstall the app or clear its storage. Account and event data are kept while your account is active. To delete everything we hold, email [YOUR EMAIL HERE] from your registered address and we will remove your account and associated events within 30 days.

Children

CardFit is not intended for anyone under 18 and we do not knowingly collect data from children.
